# Privacy Policy — Merge Blocks

_Last updated: July 6, 2026_

Merge Blocks ("the app") is a free game supported by advertising.

## Information we collect

The app itself does not collect, store, or share any personal information.
Your best score is stored only on your device.

## Advertising

The app uses **Google AdMob** to display ads. AdMob may collect and process
data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and coarse location
to serve and measure ads. See Google's policies:

- How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Users in the EEA/UK are shown a consent form (Google UMP) and can choose
non-personalized ads.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad
serving follows Google Play Families and AdMob content policies.
